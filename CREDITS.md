# Credits

**Taiga Arsenal** by IornMan1213.

- **Store models**: made for this pack by `tools/arsenal_gen.py` from the real weapons' published dimensions.
- **Weapon behaviour**: each store is built on one of Nuclear Option's own weapons (its effects, networking and guidance) and
  retuned. No game files are included in this repository or in the release; the build reads them from a local Blueprinter
  project. Nuclear Option is by Shockfront Studios.
- **[Blueprinter](https://github.com/nikkorap/NOBlueprinter-Releases)** by nikkorap: the mod format and loader, and the
  add-weapon ops that put the stores on other mods' aircraft.

## Supported aircraft mods

Taiga Arsenal only refers to these mods' aircraft by their keys and station numbers; none of their files are included or
changed.

- **MiG-29 Fulcrum** and **MiG-29SMT** by IornMan1213.
- **Su-30VXN Gandiva** by KyleisRed: <https://github.com/KyleisRed/Su-30VXN-Gandiva>
