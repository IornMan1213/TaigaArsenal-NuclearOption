# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First public release.

- **50 Soviet and Russian munitions**, each modelled at its real size and tuned to the real weapon's mass, warhead, motor and
  seeker:
  - eight GSh-30-1 cannon rounds: OFZ, OFZT, BR-T, BZT, RTP, ME, MKETF airburst and OFZT-SR;
  - six air-to-air missiles: R-27ER, R-27ET, R-74M, R-77, R-24R and R-24T;
  - thirteen air-to-surface missiles: Kh-25ML, Kh-25MP, Kh-38ML, Kh-38MT, Kh-38MK, Kh-59, Kh-59M, Kh-35U, Kh-29L, Kh-29T,
    Kh-29TD, Kh-31A and Kh-31P;
  - seventeen bombs: KAB-500S-E, KAB-250LG-E, KAB-250S-E, KAB-500L, KAB-500Kr, FAB-100, FAB-250, FAB-500, OFAB-250-270,
    ODAB-500PM, BetAB-500ShP, ZAB-500, RBK-250, RBK-500, PBK-500U Drel, KMGU-2 and RN-40;
  - five rocket stores: B-8M1 (S-8KOM), B-13L (S-13OF), UB-32 (S-5M), S-24B and S-25OF;
  - the MSP-418K jamming pod.
- **Three supported aircraft mods**, each with the stores on stations that suit it: the MiG-29 Fulcrum, the MiG-29SMT and the
  Su-30VXN Gandiva. Which station takes what: [docs/WEAPONS.md](docs/WEAPONS.md).
- Nothing in the aircraft mods is changed: remove Taiga Arsenal and the weapons are gone again.
- Release zip ready for manual install or a mod manager (the `.nobp`, its mod manager card, credits and license).
- In-game firing tests for every store, and for the Su-30VXN.
